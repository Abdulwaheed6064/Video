## Do not add

- Categories for services the business doesn't do (e.g. "Pest control" + "Plumber" to widen reach).
- Anything implying a licence or certification the owner hasn't verified.
- A storefront-style setup for a home-based business: keep the address hidden and set real service areas.
- Frequent primary-category changes: at most once per batch, marked `risk: high`.
